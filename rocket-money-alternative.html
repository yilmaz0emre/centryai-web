Why People Look for Rocket Money Alternatives

Rocket Money (formerly Truebill) is a popular bill management app, but it has real limitations that push users to look for alternatives:

  • Bank account required. Rocket Money links your bank to detect subscriptions. Many users are uncomfortable sharing bank credentials with a third-party app.
  • US-only. Rocket Money is not available outside the United States. International users have no access to it.
  • Variable pricing. Rocket Money Premium asks you to choose what you pay ($6–$12/month), which can feel opaque.
  • No zombie detection. Rocket Money shows what you pay but doesn't flag subscriptions you haven't used in months.
  • No instant cancel finder. Cancellation is human-assisted, not a one-tap AI lookup.
