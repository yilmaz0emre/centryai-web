2. YNAB — Best for Full Budgeting

YNAB (You Need A Budget)

Best Full Budgeting App

Best for: Former Mint users who want a complete budgeting system and are willing to invest time in learning zero-based budgeting.

YNAB uses a zero-based budgeting philosophy — every dollar is assigned a job before it's spent. It's more actively managed than Mint was, which means it requires more effort but produces better results for people serious about their finances. Bank linking is required; it supports major US and international banks.

Pros

  • Most powerful budgeting method
  • Strong educational resources
  • Works internationally
  • 34-day free trial

Cons

  • Steep learning curve
  • Bank account required
  • $14.99/month (no free tier)
  • No AI or auto-detection

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened to Mint?

Intuit shut down Mint on January 1, 2024. Users were redirected to Credit Karma, another Intuit product. All Mint data was permanently deleted when the service closed. Mint had been operating since 2006 and had over 3.6 million active users at its peak.

What is the best free Mint alternative?

CentryAI is the best free Mint alternative for subscription tracking — it has a permanent free plan (up to 3 subscriptions) with no bank account required. For full budgeting, YNAB offers a 34-day free trial and Monarch Money offers 7 days. No fully-featured free budgeting app has emerged to truly replace what Mint offered for free.

What is the best Mint alternative without linking a bank account?

CentryAI. It detects subscriptions by scanning your Gmail or iCloud inbox instead of your bank account — read-only email access, no banking credentials required. This makes it the most privacy-friendly Mint alternative available.

Is Credit Karma a good Mint replacement?

No. Credit Karma is a credit score monitoring service, not a budgeting or subscription tracking tool. Intuit redirected Mint users there, but it doesn't replicate Mint's core features. Most former Mint users have switched to YNAB, Monarch Money, or CentryAI depending on their needs.

Which Mint alternative works outside the US?

CentryAI works globally — 18 languages, all currencies, no US bank account needed. YNAB also has strong international bank support. Monarch Money and Copilot are currently US-only. PocketGuard has limited international coverage.

What is the closest app to Mint?

Monarch Money is the closest direct replacement for Mint's full feature set — budgeting categories, net worth, investments, and spending analysis in one app. For subscription tracking specifically, CentryAI is more powerful than Mint was, with AI detection and zombie scoring that Mint never offered.

Can I export my Mint data?

Mint's data export window closed when the service shut down on January 1, 2024. If you didn't export your transaction history before then, that data is no longer recoverable. Going forward, any new subscription tracking or budgeting tool you use will start fresh from the date you sign up.
