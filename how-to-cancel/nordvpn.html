How to Cancel NordVPN

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Cancel: nordvpn.com → log in → Billing → Subscriptions → Cancel auto-renew. Refund: Contact NordVPN live chat within 30 days of purchase to claim the money-back guarantee. After 30 days, no refunds are available.

How to Cancel NordVPN on Web

Almost all NordVPN subscriptions are billed directly, not through Apple or Google Play.

  • 1Go to nordvpn.com and log in to your account.
  • 2Click your email/account icon in the top-right corner.
  • 3Navigate to Billing → Subscriptions.
  • 4Click Cancel subscription or Turn off auto-renew next to your active plan.
  • 5Confirm. Your VPN access continues until the plan end date.
Turning off auto-renew vs. cancelling Cancelling auto-renew prevents any future charge and lets your current plan expire naturally. Your VPN works normally until the end date. If you also want a refund (within 30 days), contact live chat after turning off auto-renew.

How to Get a NordVPN Refund (30-Day Money-Back)

NordVPN offers a full refund within 30 days of your original purchase. After that window, no refunds are available.

  • 1First, turn off auto-renew in your account (steps above).
  • 2Go to nordvpn.com/contact-us/ and start a live chat.
  • 3Tell the agent you want to request a refund under the 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • 4Provide your account email. Refund is typically processed in 5–10 business days to your original payment method.

How to Cancel NordVPN on iPhone (iOS)

Only if you subscribed via the App Store (uncommon for NordVPN).

  • 1Open Settings → tap your Apple ID.
  • 2Tap Subscriptions → NordVPN → Cancel Subscription.
Most NordVPN subscriptions are direct If NordVPN doesn't appear in your Apple or Google subscriptions, cancel at nordvpn.com using the web steps above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NordVPN offer a refund when I cancel?

Yes — within 30 days of purchase. Cancel auto-renew in your account, then contact NordVPN live chat to request the refund. The 30-day money-back guarantee applies to all new subscriptions. After 30 days, no refunds are available.

How do I get a NordVPN refund?

Contact NordVPN live chat at nordvpn.com/contact-us/ within 30 days of your purchase date and request a refund under the money-back guarantee. No need to justify the reason. Refunds typically arrive in 5–10 business days.

What happens to my NordVPN account after I cancel?

Your VPN access continues until the subscription end date. After that, you can no longer connect to NordVPN servers. Your account remains open and you can resubscribe any time.

Can I cancel NordVPN mid-year without paying a fee?

Yes, within 30 days — you get a full refund. After 30 days, you can still cancel auto-renew (no fee), but NordVPN does not refund unused months beyond the initial window. You simply keep access until the plan end date.
