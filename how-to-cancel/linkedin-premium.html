How to Cancel LinkedIn Premium

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Web: linkedin.com → Me → Settings & Privacy → Subscriptions & Payments → Cancel Premium subscription → Confirm. Your profile, connections, and posts are completely unaffected. You revert to a free LinkedIn account at the end of the billing period.

How to Cancel LinkedIn Premium on Web (Browser)

This is the recommended method. Works for all LinkedIn Premium tiers (Career, Business, Sales Navigator, Recruiter Lite).

  • 1Log in to linkedin.com.
  • 2Click your profile photo (Me) in the top navigation bar.
  • 3Select Settings & Privacy from the dropdown.
  • 4In the left sidebar, click Subscriptions & Payments.
  • 5Click Cancel Premium subscription.
  • 6Follow the prompts. LinkedIn may offer 1 free month or a discount — click Confirm cancellation if you want to fully cancel.
LinkedIn may offer a retention discount During the cancel flow, LinkedIn often offers 1 free month or a significant price reduction. Accept only if you genuinely plan to use Premium features. Otherwise, confirm cancellation to stop billing.

How to Cancel LinkedIn Premium on iPhone (iOS)

Use this only if you originally subscribed through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings → tap your Apple ID.
  • 2Tap Subscriptions.
  • 3Find LinkedIn → tap Cancel Subscription → Confirm.

How to Cancel LinkedIn Premium on Android

Use this if you subscribed through the Google Play Store.

  • 1Open Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • 2Tap LinkedIn → Cancel subscription → Confirm.
Most LinkedIn Premium users are billed directly If LinkedIn doesn't appear in your Apple or Google subscriptions, cancel at linkedin.com using the web steps above.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my LinkedIn profile when I cancel Premium?

Your profile, connections, posts, and message history are fully preserved. You lose Premium-only features: InMail credits, full who-viewed-your-profile list, LinkedIn Learning, AI tools, and the gold Premium badge. Your network is not affected at all.

Can I get a refund when I cancel LinkedIn Premium?

No. LinkedIn does not issue refunds for partial billing periods. Your Premium access continues until the current billing cycle ends with no further charges.

What do I lose when I downgrade from LinkedIn Premium to free?

You lose: InMail credits, full profile viewer list (you only see viewer counts, not names), LinkedIn Learning, AI-powered profile and job features, enhanced analytics, and the gold Premium badge. All your connections, messages, and content stay intact.

How do I cancel LinkedIn Premium if I'm on an annual plan?

Annual plans follow the same cancellation steps. No early termination fee — LinkedIn annual plans do not charge a cancellation fee. You lose Premium access at the end of the annual period you already paid for.
