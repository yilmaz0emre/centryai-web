How to Cancel Duolingo Super

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Web: duolingo.com → profile → Settings → Super Duolingo → Cancel Subscription. iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Duolingo → Cancel (if subscribed via App Store). Your streak and all language progress are completely safe — only Premium features are removed.

How to Cancel Duolingo Super on Web (Browser)

This works if you subscribed directly through Duolingo's website.

  • 1Go to duolingo.com and log in.
  • 2Click your profile icon (top-right) → Settings.
  • 3Select Super Duolingo in the left menu (or go to duolingo.com/settings/billing).
  • 4Click Cancel Subscription.
  • 5Follow the prompts and confirm. Your Super features continue until the billing period ends.
Duolingo Super was formerly called Duolingo Plus If your subscription shows as "Duolingo Plus," the cancellation steps are identical. Duolingo rebranded Plus to Super Duolingo in 2023.

How to Cancel Duolingo on iPhone (iOS)

Use this if you subscribed through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID.
  • 2Tap Subscriptions.
  • 3Tap Duolingo (may appear as "Duolingo Super" or "Duolingo Plus").
  • 4Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

How to Cancel Duolingo on Android

Use this if you subscribed through the Google Play Store.

  • 1Open Google Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • 2Tap Duolingo → Cancel subscription.
  • 3Follow the prompts to confirm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my streak if I cancel Duolingo Super?

No. Your streak, XP, crowns, and all language progress are permanently stored on your Duolingo account. Cancelling Super removes premium features (ad-free, streak shields, unlimited hearts on iOS) but your progress is never touched.

What is the difference between Duolingo Super and Duolingo Plus?

They are the same product — Duolingo renamed Plus to Super in 2023. If your subscription still shows as Plus, cancellation works identically. Both names refer to the same paid tier.

What do I lose when I cancel Duolingo Super?

You lose: ad-free learning, unlimited hearts on iOS, streak repair credits (Streak Society), monthly gems bonus, and offline lessons. You keep all your language progress, XP, streak count, and can continue learning for free indefinitely.

Can I get a refund when I cancel Duolingo?

Duolingo doesn't offer refunds for partial billing periods directly. For App Store purchases, you can request a refund through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com within 90 days of purchase. For Google Play, use Google Play's refund request within the eligible window.
