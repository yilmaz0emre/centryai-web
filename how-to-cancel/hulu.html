How to Cancel Hulu

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Web: hulu.com → profile icon → Account → Your Subscription → Cancel → Continue to Cancel. iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Hulu → Cancel (only if subscribed via App Store). No cancellation fee. Access continues to the end of your billing period.

How to Cancel Hulu on Web (Browser)

This method works for all Hulu plans — Hulu basic, No Ads, and Hulu + Live TV.

  • 1Go to hulu.com and sign in to your account.
  • 2Click your profile name in the top-right corner.
  • 3Select Account from the dropdown.
  • 4Under Your Subscription, click Cancel.
  • 5Hulu may offer to pause your subscription. Click Continue to Cancel to proceed with full cancellation.
  • 6Confirm your cancellation on the final screen.
Tip: Pause instead of cancel Hulu offers a pause option (up to 12 weeks) so you keep your account without being charged. If you're only pausing temporarily, pausing is better than cancelling since your watchlist and history are preserved and accessible immediately when you unpause.

How to Cancel Hulu on iPhone (iOS)

Use this method if you subscribed through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • 2Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top.
  • 3Tap Subscriptions.
  • 4Find and tap Hulu.
  • 5Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

How to Cancel Hulu on Android

Use this method if you subscribed via Google Play.

  • 1Open the Google Play Store.
  • 2Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • 3Tap Hulu → Cancel subscription.
  • 4Confirm cancellation.
Not seeing Hulu in Play subscriptions? Most Hulu users are billed directly by Hulu. Cancel at hulu.com using the web steps above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund when I cancel Hulu?

No. Hulu does not issue refunds for partially used billing periods. Your Hulu access continues until the end of the billing cycle you already paid for, with no further charges.

How do I cancel Hulu + Live TV specifically?

Use the same web cancellation steps above — hulu.com → Account → Your Subscription → Cancel. This cancels your entire plan including Live TV. If you only want to remove an add-on (like ESPN+), go to Manage Add-ons instead.

Can I pause Hulu instead of cancelling?

Yes. During the cancellation flow, Hulu offers a pause option for up to 12 weeks. You won't be billed during the pause and your watchlist is preserved. It's the best option if you're planning to return.

What happens to my Hulu watchlist after I cancel?

Your watchlist and viewing history are saved for 12 months. If you reactivate within that time, everything is restored. After 12 months the account is permanently deleted.
