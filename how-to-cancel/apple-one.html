How to Cancel Apple One

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Apple One → Cancel Subscription. Mac: Apple menu → System Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Apple One → Cancel. There is no web cancellation for Apple One. After cancellation, all included services (Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, iCloud+) stop at the billing period end.

⚠️ Check Your iCloud Storage Before Cancelling If your Apple One plan includes iCloud+ storage (50GB, 200GB, or 2TB), cancelling Apple One will drop your iCloud storage to the free 5GB tier. If your iCloud is over 5GB, you won't be able to upload new content. Subscribe to iCloud+ separately if you need to keep your storage.

How to Cancel Apple One on iPhone (iOS)

This is the only way to cancel Apple One — there is no website or browser-based cancellation option.

  • 1Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • 2Tap your name (Apple ID) at the very top of the Settings screen.
  • 3Tap Subscriptions.
  • 4Find and tap Apple One in the list.
  • 5Tap Cancel Subscription at the bottom of the screen.
  • 6Confirm. All Apple One services continue until the end of the billing period, then stop.
Don't see Apple One in Subscriptions? Make sure you're signed in with the correct Apple ID — the one that was used when subscribing to Apple One. Apple One appears under the account that manages it, not under family members' accounts.

How to Cancel Apple One on Mac

  • 1Click the Apple menu (top-left) → System Settings.
  • 2Click your Apple ID name at the top of the sidebar.
  • 3Click Subscriptions.
  • 4Click Manage next to Apple One → Cancel Subscription.

What Happens to Each Service After Cancellation

When Apple One ends, each bundled service stops individually. Here's what to expect:

  • 🎵Apple Music — Access ends. Playlists and saved songs remain in the app but cannot be played without a subscription.
  • 📺Apple TV+ — Access ends. Downloaded content stops playing.
  • 🎮Apple Arcade — Access ends. Games become unplayable but progress is saved if you resubscribe.
  • ☁️iCloud+ — Storage drops to 5GB. If over 5GB, uploads pause until you free space or resubscribe to iCloud+.
  • 📰Apple News+ (Premier plan) — Access ends. Free Apple News articles remain accessible.
  • 🏃Apple Fitness+ (Premier plan) — Access ends. Workout history is preserved in the Health app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel Apple One on the web or through apple.com?

No. Apple One can only be cancelled through device Settings — iPhone, iPad, or Mac. There is no website-based cancellation. The steps are: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Apple One → Cancel Subscription.

What happens to my iCloud storage when I cancel Apple One?

Your iCloud storage drops to the free 5GB tier at the end of the billing period. If you're using more than 5GB, you won't be able to upload new files or photos to iCloud. Download local backups before cancelling, and consider subscribing to iCloud+ separately if you need the storage.

Does cancelling Apple One affect my family members?

Yes, for Family and Premier plans. All family members lose access to the shared services (Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, iCloud+ shared storage) when the plan ends. Each member would need to subscribe individually to keep any services they use.

Can I get a refund when I cancel Apple One?

Apple does not automatically offer refunds for unused time. However, if you have a recent charge, you can request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com within 90 days of the purchase date. Go to the site, find the Apple One charge, and request a refund.
