How to Cancel Netflix

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

On web: netflix.com → profile icon → Account → Cancel Membership → Finish Cancellation. On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Netflix → Cancel (only if subscribed via App Store). No cancellation fees. Access continues to the end of your billing period.

How to Cancel Netflix on Web (Browser)

This method works for all Netflix accounts billed directly by Netflix (credit card, PayPal, or gift card).

  • 1Go to netflix.com and sign in to your account.
  • 2Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the page.
  • 3Select Account from the dropdown menu.
  • 4Scroll to the Membership & Billing section and click Cancel Membership.
  • 5Click Continue, then Finish Cancellation to confirm. Done — no further charges will occur.
What happens next Your Netflix account stays active and fully watchable until the end of the current billing period. After that, access stops automatically. No partial refunds are issued for unused days.

How to Cancel Netflix on iPhone (iOS)

Use this method only if you originally subscribed to Netflix through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  • 2Tap your name (Apple ID) at the very top of the Settings screen.
  • 3Tap Subscriptions.
  • 4Find and tap Netflix in the list.
  • 5Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm when prompted.
Don't see Netflix in Subscriptions? If Netflix doesn't appear under your Apple subscriptions, you subscribed directly through Netflix. Use the web method above instead.

How to Cancel Netflix on Android

Use this method if you subscribed through the Google Play Store.

  • 1Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  • 2Tap your profile icon (top-right) → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • 3Tap Netflix in the list.
  • 4Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts to confirm.
Most Netflix users aren't billed through Google Play Netflix typically bills directly. If you don't see it in Play subscriptions, cancel at netflix.com using the web steps above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund when I cancel Netflix?

No. Netflix does not offer refunds for partially used billing periods. After cancelling, your account remains active until the current billing cycle ends — you just won't be charged again.

What happens to my Netflix profile when I cancel?

Your profile, watch history, and My List are saved for 10 months. If you reactivate within that period, everything is automatically restored exactly as you left it.

Can I cancel Netflix at any time without a fee?

Yes. Netflix has no contracts and no early cancellation fees. You can cancel any time — your subscription simply continues to the end of the paid period before stopping.

How do I know which billing method Netflix uses for my account?

Go to netflix.com → Account → Membership & Billing. Your billing method (credit card, PayPal, Apple, Google, etc.) is shown there. If it says Apple or Google, use the app-based cancellation method.
