How to Cancel Adobe Creative Cloud

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 5 min read

Quick Answer

Web: account.adobe.com → Plans & Products → Manage Plan → Cancel Plan. Critical warning: Annual plans billed monthly carry a 50% early termination fee on remaining months if you cancel after the first 14 days. Annual plans paid upfront get a pro-rata refund with no fee.

⚠️ Know Your Plan Before You Cancel If you have an annual plan paid monthly (e.g., $59.99/month billed for 12 months), cancelling early triggers a fee equal to 50% of your remaining monthly charges. This is waived only in the first 14 days. Annual plans paid upfront (one lump sum) receive a pro-rata refund with no termination fee. Month-to-month plans have no fee — ever.

How to Cancel Adobe Creative Cloud on Web

Most Adobe subscriptions are billed directly by Adobe. This is the primary cancellation method.

  • 1Go to account.adobe.com and sign in.
  • 2Click Plans & Products in the left navigation panel.
  • 3Find your Creative Cloud plan and click Manage Plan.
  • 4Click Cancel Plan. Adobe will show you any applicable early termination fee before you confirm.
  • 5Complete the survey and click Confirm Cancellation.
Adobe may offer a discount first Adobe often shows a 40–50% discount offer before you reach the final cancellation confirmation. If you only want to reduce cost rather than cancel, consider accepting it. If you want to fully cancel, click "No thanks" or "Continue to cancel."

How to Cancel Adobe on iPhone (iOS)

Only applies if you originally subscribed via the App Store — rare for Creative Cloud.

  • 1Open Settings → tap your Apple ID.
  • 2Tap Subscriptions → find Adobe.
  • 3Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.
Most Adobe users cancel at account.adobe.com Creative Cloud is almost always billed directly by Adobe, not through Apple or Google. If you don't see Adobe in your Apple or Google subscriptions, cancel at account.adobe.com.

Cancelling Without an Early Termination Fee — Your Options

If you're past the 14-day free-cancel window on an annual plan billed monthly, here are your alternatives:

  • 1Pay the fee and cancel now. Sometimes the fee is less painful than 8 more months of charges you don't want.
  • 2Wait until renewal. Switch off auto-renew now, then your subscription expires at the annual renewal date with no fee. To turn off auto-renew: account.adobe.com → Plans & Products → Manage Plan → Turn Off Auto-Renew.
  • 3Accept the retention offer. Adobe often offers 40–50% off for 3 months during the cancel flow — a significant saving if you still need the apps occasionally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Adobe charge a cancellation fee?

Yes, for annual plans billed monthly if cancelled after 14 days. The fee is 50% of remaining monthly charges. No fee applies for: month-to-month plans, annual plans paid upfront, or cancellations within the first 14 days of any plan.

How do I avoid the Adobe early termination fee?

Either cancel within 14 days of signup (full refund), wait until the end of your annual period (turn off auto-renew instead), or switch to a month-to-month plan first (though this will cost more per month). Paying the annual plan upfront also avoids any fee.

Can I use Photoshop after I cancel Adobe?

No. Paid Creative Cloud apps stop working after the billing period ends. You can still open files locally but cannot edit them in Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, etc. Download and export any critical files before cancelling.

What happens to my Adobe cloud storage when I cancel?

You have a 30-day grace period after cancellation to download files stored in Adobe Creative Cloud cloud storage. After 30 days, Adobe may delete cloud-only files. Always back up important files locally before cancelling.
