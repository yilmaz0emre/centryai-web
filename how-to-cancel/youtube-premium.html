How to Cancel YouTube Premium

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Web: youtube.com/paid_memberships → Manage → Deactivate. iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → YouTube Premium → Cancel (if subscribed via App Store). Note: Cancelling YouTube Premium also cancels YouTube Music Premium — they share the same subscription.

How to Cancel YouTube Premium on Web (Browser)

This is the fastest method and works regardless of how you originally subscribed.

  • 1Go to youtube.com/paid_memberships and sign in with your Google account.
  • 2Find YouTube Premium and click Manage.
  • 3Click Deactivate (at the bottom of the page).
  • 4Follow the confirmation prompts. Your Premium access continues until the end of the billing period.
YouTube Music Premium is included YouTube Premium bundles YouTube Music Premium at no extra cost. Cancelling YouTube Premium removes your ad-free YouTube Music access at the same time. If you want to keep Music Premium, you'd need a separate YouTube Music subscription.

How to Cancel YouTube Premium on iPhone (iOS)

Use this if you subscribed through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • 2Tap your Apple ID name at the top.
  • 3Tap Subscriptions.
  • 4Tap YouTube Premium → Cancel Subscription → Confirm.

How to Cancel YouTube Premium on Android

Use this if you subscribed through Google Play, or cancel directly in the YouTube app.

  • 1Open the YouTube app on your Android device.
  • 2Tap your profile icon (top-right) → Purchases and memberships.
  • 3Tap Manage membership → Deactivate.
  • 4Alternative: Open Google Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → YouTube Premium → Cancel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does cancelling YouTube Premium also cancel YouTube Music?

Yes. YouTube Premium includes YouTube Music Premium. Cancelling the premium plan removes both YouTube ad-free viewing and YouTube Music Premium. They cannot be separated — it's a single bundled subscription.

Can I get a refund when I cancel YouTube Premium?

Google does not offer refunds for partially used billing periods on YouTube Premium. After cancelling, you keep access until the current billing cycle ends and are not charged again.

What happens to my YouTube Premium downloads when I cancel?

Downloaded videos are stored on your device but become unplayable once your Premium subscription ends. The files remain on your storage but the YouTube app won't play them without an active Premium membership.

How do I cancel YouTube Premium on a family plan?

Only the family plan manager can cancel the entire plan. Members can leave the family group without cancelling it. The manager cancels at youtube.com/paid_memberships using the same web steps above.
