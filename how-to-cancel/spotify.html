How to Cancel Spotify Premium

Last updated: June 11, 2026 · By CentryAI Team · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Web: spotify.com/account → Subscription → Change Plan → Cancel Premium. iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Spotify → Cancel. You revert to the free plan at the end of the billing period — your playlists and saved music are never deleted.

How to Cancel Spotify Premium on Web (Browser)

This is the fastest method. Works for all direct Spotify billing (card, PayPal). You can cancel even if billed through PayPal.

  • 1Go to spotify.com/account and log in.
  • 2Click Subscription in the left-hand menu, or scroll to Your plan.
  • 3Click Change Plan (or Manage Plan).
  • 4Scroll to the bottom of the plan options and click Cancel Premium.
  • 5Follow the confirmation steps. Spotify may show a discount offer — decline if you want to cancel fully.
What happens after cancellation Your Premium plan continues until the end of the current billing period. Then your account automatically reverts to Spotify Free — no ads-removed, no offline listening, shuffle-only on mobile. All your playlists and saved music remain intact.

How to Cancel Spotify on iPhone (iOS)

Use this method only if you originally subscribed through the Apple App Store.

  • 1Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • 2Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top.
  • 3Tap Subscriptions.
  • 4Tap Spotify in the list.
  • 5Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

How to Cancel Spotify on Android

Use this method if you subscribed through the Google Play Store.

  • 1Open the Google Play Store app.
  • 2Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • 3Tap Spotify → Cancel subscription.
  • 4Follow the prompts to confirm cancellation.

How to Cancel Spotify via PayPal

If Spotify bills you through PayPal, you can stop automatic payments directly from PayPal.

  • 1Log in to paypal.com.
  • 2Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right.
  • 3Go to Payments → Manage Automatic Payments.
  • 4Find Spotify and click Cancel.
Tip: Cancel at Spotify directly instead You can also cancel at spotify.com/account even if you pay via PayPal — it's faster and more reliable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my music when I cancel Spotify Premium?

Nothing is deleted. All your playlists, liked songs, albums, and podcasts remain. You move to the free plan — ads come back, offline listening stops, and mobile playback becomes shuffle-only. Resubscribing restores all Premium features instantly.

Can I get a refund when I cancel Spotify?

No. Spotify does not offer refunds for partial billing periods. After cancelling, you keep Premium access until the end of the billing cycle with no further charges.

How do I cancel Spotify Premium if I pay through PayPal?

Cancel at spotify.com/account → Subscription → Change Plan → Cancel Premium. This works regardless of how you pay. Alternatively, cancel automatic payments through PayPal settings under Payments → Manage Automatic Payments.

Does cancelling Spotify Premium delete my playlists?

No. Your playlists and saved music are permanently stored on your Spotify account. Cancelling Premium only removes Premium features — your library is never touched.
