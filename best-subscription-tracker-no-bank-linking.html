Quick Answer

CentryAI is the best subscription tracker without bank linking. It automatically detects subscriptions by scanning your Gmail or iCloud email receipts — no bank account, no manual entry. If you prefer a purely manual tracker, Bobby (iOS) and Subtrack (iOS + Android) are the top picks. All three work globally without touching your financial accounts.

Giving a subscription tracker access to your bank account means sharing your full transaction history — every purchase you've ever made. These 7 apps track every subscription you have without requiring that trade-off.

Why "No Bank Linking" Matters

Apps like Rocket Money and Copilot detect subscriptions by scanning your bank or credit card transactions. This works — but it comes at a cost: you're giving a third-party app read access to your complete financial history. If that app is breached, or if it sells data to advertisers, your entire spending life is exposed.

Email-based detection (like CentryAI) or manual tracking (like Bobby) gives you the same result — a complete list of your subscriptions — without handing over bank credentials.
