Why People Look for Copilot Alternatives

  • US-only. Copilot connects to US banks via Plaid and is unavailable outside the United States. International users cannot use it at all.
  • Bank account required. Copilot's core functionality relies on bank and credit card access. Users who don't want to share financial credentials have no option with Copilot.
  • Budgeting app, not subscription-specific. Copilot tracks all spending, not just subscriptions. This makes it heavier than needed for users who only want to manage recurring charges.
  • No free tier. Copilot is $8.99/month after a 14-day trial. There is no free plan for continued use.
  • No zombie detection or cancel finder. Copilot doesn't flag subscriptions you're paying for but not using, and doesn't provide one-tap cancel page lookups.
