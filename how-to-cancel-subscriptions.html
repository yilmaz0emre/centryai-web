How to Cancel a Subscription: 5 Steps

1

Find the cancellation page

Log in to the service's website. Look for Account → Subscription, Account → Billing, or Settings → Manage Plan. Most subscription services have a cancel option in one of these locations.

Shortcut: Use CentryAI's Cancel Finder — it uses AI to find the exact cancel URL for 90+ services instantly, including ones that bury the option deep in their settings.
2

Cancel on the website first (not your bank)

Always try to cancel directly with the service. Disputing a charge with your bank ("chargeback") without cancelling first may not prevent future charges and can flag your account.

3

For app-billed subscriptions: cancel via app store

iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → select the subscription → Cancel Subscription.
Android: Google Play → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → select → Cancel.

Important: if you subscribed through the app store, you must cancel there — cancelling on the company's website alone won't stop the charge.

4

Dealing with deliberately hard cancellations

Some companies (gyms, cable providers, certain SaaS tools) require phone calls, live chat, or certified mail to cancel. This is a dark pattern designed to create friction. If online cancellation isn't possible: call customer service, say clearly "I want to cancel my subscription effective immediately," and don't accept retention offers unless genuinely useful.

5

Confirm cancellation in writing

Every cancellation should result in a confirmation email. If you don't receive one within 1 hour, log back in and verify the subscription status shows as cancelled. Keep the confirmation email for your records.
