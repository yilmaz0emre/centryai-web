# Pricing — CentryAI Subscription Tracker

## Free
- Price: $0/month — forever free
- Limits: Up to 3 subscriptions, manual entry only
- Features: Manual subscription tracking, basic renewal reminders, multi-currency support

## Pro Monthly
- Price: $7.99/month
- Limits: Unlimited subscriptions
- Features:
  - Unlimited subscription tracking
  - AI email scanning (Gmail + iCloud inbox detection)
  - Zombie subscription detection (flags unused subscriptions)
  - Cancel Finder (AI-powered one-tap cancellation path)
  - Spending analytics with category breakdowns
  - Renewal reminders with custom notification schedule
  - Multi-currency support
  - 18-language interface
  - 7-day free trial

## Pro Annual
- Price: $59.99/year ($5.00/month equivalent — save 37% vs monthly)
- Limits: Unlimited subscriptions
- Features: All Pro Monthly features
- Notes: Best value option; 7-day free trial included

## Notes
- No credit card required for Free plan
- Trial: 7-day free trial on Pro plans (iOS/Android in-app purchase)
- Platform: iOS App Store and Google Play
- No bank account linking required at any tier
- All tiers include: privacy-first data handling, email content never stored server-side
