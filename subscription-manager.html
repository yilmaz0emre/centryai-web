Subscription Manager vs Bill Tracker vs Budgeting App

These terms overlap but they're not the same thing:

  • Subscription manager — focuses on recurring charges specifically. Detects, tracks renewal dates, scores usage, and facilitates cancellation. CentryAI.
  • Bill tracker app — tracks bills due each month (rent, utilities, subscriptions, loan payments). Broader than subscription management. Often requires manual entry.
  • Recurring payment tracker — tracks any payment that repeats on a schedule. Includes both subscriptions and non-subscription bills (insurance, mortgage).
  • Budgeting app — tracks all spending across all categories. Includes subscriptions as one category among many. YNAB, Monarch Money.

CentryAI is primarily a subscription manager — it goes deeper on recurring subscriptions than any budgeting app, and catches more than any manual bill tracker because it auto-detects from your inbox.
